RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (EXCERPT)
Premises: 1200 Harbor Street, Unit 4B, Eastport, CA 94701
1. PARTIES. This Lease Agreement is entered into between Harborview
Residential LP ("Landlord") and Dana Okafor ("Tenant").
2. TERM. The initial term of this Lease shall be twenty-four (24)
months, commencing on March 1, 2025 and ending on February 28, 2027,
unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement.
3. RENT. Tenant shall pay Landlord monthly rent of $2,350.00, due on
the first day of each calendar month. Rent received after the fifth
day of the month is subject to a late charge of $75.00.
4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Upon execution of this Agreement, Tenant shall
deposit with Landlord the sum of $3,525.00 as a security deposit,
to be held and disbursed in accordance with applicable law.
5. UTILITIES. Tenant is responsible for electricity, gas, and
internet service. Landlord pays for water, sewer, and refuse
collection.
6. OCCUPANCY. The Premises shall be occupied solely by Tenant and
one additional registered occupant. Subletting requires Landlord's
prior written consent.
