EASTPORT RENTAL MARKET REPORT (EXCERPT)
Prepared by Shoreline Market Analytics
Reporting Period: Q3 2025
Market Area: Eastport-Harborview Submarket
MARKET OVERVIEW
The Eastport-Harborview Submarket continued to tighten through the
third quarter. The overall vacancy rate declined to 4.2%, down from
4.9% in the prior quarter, driven by steady absorption of the two
projects delivered in late 2024 and no new completions this quarter.
Average asking rent for a one-bedroom unit reached $1,985 per month,
a year-over-year increase of 3.8%. Two-bedroom units averaged $2,540
per month over the same period. Concessions remain limited to
select lease-ups, with most stabilized properties offering none.
SUPPLY
No new units were delivered in Q3. One project of 96 units at
Dockside Commons remains under construction, with completion
expected in Q2 2026. No additional projects have broken ground
this year, suggesting constrained supply through at least 2027.
OUTLOOK
Shoreline Market Analytics expects vacancy to remain below 5%
through the next four quarters, with rent growth moderating toward
3% as affordability pressure builds at the upper end of the market.
